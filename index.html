AboutDr. Manisha Raut

Female Laser Piles Surgeon in Indore

Dr. Manisha Raut, a leading Female Laser Piles Surgeon in Indore, practices at Intimate Clinic, offering advanced laser treatments for piles, fissures, fistulas, and constipation. She holds an MS in General Surgery from Nasik University and completed a fellowship in proctology under Dr. Ashwin Porwal. With extensive experience across Maharashtra, she specializes in minimally invasive procedures like Laser Haemorrhoidoplasty (LHP), MIPH, and STARR for rectal prolapse. Known for her empathetic, patient-centric approach, she is especially trusted by female patients seeking expert and sensitive care.
